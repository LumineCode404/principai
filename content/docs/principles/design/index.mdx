title: Design
description: Simplicity, judgment about duplication and speculation, and the laws that govern measures and surprises.
---

Design principles carry the lowest severities in the library — mostly advisory — because they are about the long-term cost of complexity, not the immediate blast radius of a failure. They still bite: a wrong abstraction or a gamed metric compounds quietly until it is the most expensive thing in the system.
