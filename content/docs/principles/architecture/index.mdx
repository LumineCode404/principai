---
title: Architecture
description: How systems and organizations shape each other — boundaries, dependencies, and growth from simple beginnings.
---

Architecture principles describe forces, not blueprints. Conway's Law and Gall's Law are observations about what organizations and time actually produce; single responsibility and dependency inversion are disciplines for keeping the core replaceable. None of them promise a perfect design — they bias the system toward survivable change.
